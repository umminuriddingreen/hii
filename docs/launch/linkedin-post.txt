I have been building HII because I do not want my work trapped in a stack of disconnected files, tools, and AI chats.

HII is a visual workspace where notes, files, websites, images, models, chats, and tools can live together.

You can arrange the work like Miro or Figma, then ask HII to help you understand it, change it, or make something new. The context you approve, the result, and the proof stay connected on the workspace.

This video uses the actual interface—not a concept render.
