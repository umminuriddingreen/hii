# Architectural Deliverable Standards

## General

Derive every view from the same canonical design-state version. Put project name, location, phase, option, date, author/agent, scale status, units, source model version, sheet number, revision, and professional-use disclaimer on formal sheets.

Use conventional line hierarchy and restrained color. Distinguish existing, demolition, proposed, overhead, hidden, cut, and illustrative work consistently.

## Plans

Show as appropriate: property/site context, north, scale, levels, grids, walls, openings with swings, glazing, rooms, key dimensions, clearances, fixtures/equipment, circulation, structural elements, roof/overhead lines, section/elevation references, tags, notes, legends, and area summary. Dimension only established geometry and mark assumptions.

## Sections And Elevations

Show datums, grade, floors, roofs, ceiling/service zones, openings, structure, material boundaries, critical heights, neighboring context where relevant, and plan-cut references. Check every opening and level against plan and 3D state.

## Schedules And Models

Generate schedules from semantic objects and stable IDs. Provide an editable/exact model when geometry matters and a lightweight browser model for inspection. Validate that 3D views are nonblank, framed, navigable, and consistent with drawings.

## Boards And Slides

Connect project/site facts, design thesis, plan/section/massing diagrams, attributed precedents, material/environmental strategy, option metrics/tradeoffs, unresolved issues, and next decision. Avoid a collage that cannot explain why the architecture follows from the site and brief.

## Issue Log

Record stable ID, category, severity, status, description, object IDs, evidence/source IDs, owner, next action, and disposition. Use `critical`, `major`, `minor`, or `advisory`. Unknown is a valid status and is more professional than an unsupported pass.
