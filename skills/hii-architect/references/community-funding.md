# Community Funding And Transaction Boundaries

Use this protocol whenever an architectural project collects market signals, reservations, lease interest, contractor bids, sponsorship, loans, equity, revenue share, or other capital interest.

## Separate Signal Types

Never combine these into one generic commitment:

- `feedback`: non-binding opinion about design or neighborhood impact.
- `interest`: non-binding expression of possible future demand.
- `reservation`: may create consumer, refund, disclosure, and escrow obligations.
- `bid`: contractor/vendor proposal under a defined scope and validity period.
- `lease_or_prelease`: real-estate agreement requiring verified authority, premises, terms, disclosures, and applicable representation/licensing.
- `sponsorship_or_donation`: funds or in-kind support with stated consideration and tax treatment.
- `loan`: debt instrument with repayment, security, and lending-law implications.
- `equity_or_revenue_share`: ownership or return-linked interest that may be a regulated securities offering.

Default all QR responses to non-binding feedback or interest until counsel approves another lane. Display that status before submission and store the exact disclosure version accepted by the respondent.

## Required Gating Record

Before activating a transactional lane, record:

- verified property ownership and authority
- entity and responsible operator
- project/permit status and exact premises or scope
- binding versus non-binding status
- money movement, refund, escrow, and cancellation rules
- eligibility and geographic restrictions
- required broker, contractor, attorney, securities, lending, or investment-adviser review
- fair-housing, advertising, privacy, accessibility, and consumer-protection review
- risk disclosures, conflicts, compensation, and use of proceeds
- document version, reviewer, approval date, and expiration

If any required gate is unknown, collect no money and create no binding commitment.

## Architectural Data Boundary

Keep design facts separate from commercial claims. Label area as gross, net, usable, rentable, or illustrative and name the measurement standard. Link every marketed space to the current model version and approval status. Never market concept geometry as approved, deliverable, or guaranteed.

## QR Project Interface

Show source-backed project status, current option, material assumptions, design tradeoffs, privacy terms, and the non-binding nature of feedback. Let respondents choose a specific lane and consent scope. Preserve timestamps and disclosure versions. Do not expose respondent identities or financial interest publicly by default.

## Professional Boundary

Route leases, deposits, escrow, investment solicitation, securities classification, underwriting, brokerage, fair housing, tax claims, and offering documents to qualified professionals under written scopes. HII may organize evidence and workflows; it must not silently become the broker, architect of record, attorney, escrow holder, or investment adviser.
