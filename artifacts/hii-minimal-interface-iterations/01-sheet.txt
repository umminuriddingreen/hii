Minimal desktop interface concept for HII, a human information interface. The whole image is one warm-white paper-like spatial surface, calm and nearly empty. A short typed note, one small cropped architectural photograph, and a tiny three-line schedule rest directly on the surface without cards, windows, borders, panels, or shadows. A human has underlined one phrase with an imperfect graphite pencil stroke. From that underline, a very faint light-graphite model response grows naturally into the nearby margin as two concise annotations and one tiny finished artifact. The model feels attentive but invisible: no avatar, no chatbot, no assistant pane. Pencil-and-paper simplicity, editorial Swiss typography, generous negative space, tactile off-white fibers, monochrome graphite with one tiny quiet cobalt-blue proof dot. Product UI design study, straight-on desktop landscape composition. Avoid dashboard, toolbar, sidebar, dock, floating cards, chat bubbles, glassmorphism, gradients, device mockup, dense text, decorative icons, neon, sci-fi interface, 3D perspective. Only the tiny word "hii" may appear as a quiet mark in the upper-left.
