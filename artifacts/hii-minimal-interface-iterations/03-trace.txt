Minimal HII interface study where a person and a local language model work on the same quiet sheet. One warm off-white spatial canvas, almost no chrome. A source excerpt sits directly on the page. Darker graphite human marks circle and connect a few phrases. Lighter graphite model traces emerge beside those exact marks: a compact three-step plan, a small transformed artifact, and tiny source/proof ticks. The interface communicates source to thought to useful thing through placement and traces, never through a conversation window. Human intent is visibly darker; model response is visibly lighter; one tiny cobalt-blue dot indicates verified output. Refined but natural pencil-and-paper feeling, restrained editorial typesetting, huge negative space, direct manipulation, straight-on desktop landscape UI concept. Avoid cards, panels, dashboard, sidebar, toolbar, chat bubbles, avatars, glass effects, gradients, 3D perspective, device mockup, complex menus, colorful icons, dense tiny text, futuristic HUD.
