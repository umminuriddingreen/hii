Extremely minimal desktop interface concept for HII. Begin with an almost blank warm-white spatial sheet. Show only a blinking insertion point, a short line of information typed directly onto empty space, a small placed image, and one date. A rough pencil boundary drawn around the information is the steering gesture. Right beside the active boundary, the local model offers one understated useful next action as a faint graphite line, then leaves a tiny generated artifact in place. No persistent command bar and no separate AI area: the model lives in the reaction between information and space. Pencil-and-paper simplicity, quiet editorial typography, warm paper fibers, graphite and off-black with a single subdued cobalt proof mark, vast negative space, straight-on landscape product UI. Avoid windows, cards, panels, dashboard, toolbar, sidebar, dock, chat interface, icons, glossy surfaces, glassmorphism, gradients, neon, 3D perspective, device frame, clutter, ornamental graphics.
