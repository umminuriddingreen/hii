# Mies van der Rohe Architectural Reference Collection

## Purpose
Curated visual references capturing Mies van der Rohe's core architectural principles:
- "Less is more" minimalism
- Structural honesty (exposed steel/concrete)
- Free plan/facade through column grids
- Material richness (marble, glass, chrome)
- Universal space concepts

## Key Works Referenced
1. Barcelona Pavilion (1929) - Fluid spatial flow
2. Seagram Building (1958) - Bronze/glass tower typology
3. Farnsworth House (1951) - Glass pavilion in nature
4. Crown Hall (1974) - Exposed steel truss museum
5. Tugendhat House (1930) - Marble/chrome luxury modernism

## Usage
Use these SVGs as study references for:
- Spatial composition analysis
- Material palette studies
- Structural system diagrams
- Proportion/grid systems
