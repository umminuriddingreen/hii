# Cafe Vlog Chaos -> 7-Day Content Pack

**Goal:** Transform raw "cafe vlog" vibes into a cohesive 7-day social media/content calendar.
**Theme:** Slow Living / Architectural Aesthetics / Coffee Culture
**Target Audience:** Design enthusiasts, remote workers, coffee lovers.

---

## Day 1: The Arrival & Atmosphere
* **Visuals:** Wide shots of the cafe exterior, door opening, steam rising from a cup, ambient noise capture.
* **Caption Idea:** "Found my quiet corner today. ☕️✨ #SlowLiving #CafeVibes"
* **Actionable Asset:** Short loop video (5-8s) of pouring milk or stirring coffee.

## Day 2: Details & Textures
* **Visuals:** Close-ups of pastries, menu typography, wood grain tables, light filtering through windows.
* **Caption Idea:** "Details matter. Even in a latte art swirl. 🥐📸 #DesignInspo"
* **Actionable Asset:** Carousel post with high-res detail shots.

## Day 3: Work Mode / Setup
* **Visuals:** Laptop setup on the table, notebook sketches, headphones, organized desk space.
* **Caption Idea:** "Remote work essentials: Good wifi, better coffee. 💻☕ #DigitalNomad"
* **Actionable Asset:** Time-lapse of working + final shot of finished page/screen.

## Day 4: People & Interaction
* **Visuals:** Barista making drinks (hands only), smiling at camera, other patrons reading/working (blurred background).
* **Caption Idea:** "The baristas here are artists too. Respect the craft. 👨‍🎨☕ #CoffeeArt"
* **Actionable Asset:** Reel/TikTok style clip focusing on movement and skill.

## Day 5: The View Outside
* **Visuals:** Street view from window, pedestrians passing by, weather changes (rain/sun), street cats if applicable.
* **Caption Idea:** "People watching > Social media scrolling today. 🌧️👀 #UrbanVibes"
* **Actionable Asset:** Static image or slow-pan video emphasizing contrast between inside/outside.

## Day 6: Reflections & Notes
* **Visuals:** Handwritten notes in a journal, empty cup, sunset light hitting the table, packing up bag.
* **Caption Idea:** "Ideas flow best when it's quiet. What did you create this week? ✍️💭 #CreativeProcess"
* **Actionable Asset:** Photo dump of the day's highlights + text overlay quote.

## Day 7: Recap / Best Of
* **Visuals:** Montage of previous days' clips, favorite moments, final smile/cheers to camera.
* **Caption Idea:** "A week well spent at [Cafe Name]. Thanks for hanging out with me! ❤️☕ #WeeklyRecap"
* **Actionable Asset:** Compilation Reel/TikTok summarizing the week's aesthetic journey.

---

### Production Notes:
1. **Audio:** Use lo-fi beats or ambient cafe sounds (no copyright music).
2. **Color Grade:** Warm tones, slightly desaturated greens/blues for that cozy 'cafe' look.
3. **Format:** Mix vertical (9:16) for Stories/Reels and square (1:1) for Feed posts.
4. **Engagement:** Ask questions in captions related to coffee preferences or remote work setups.
