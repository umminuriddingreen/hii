Minimal desktop interface concept for HII, understood as information and space. Show a wide open warm-white information field with enormous breathing room. Only three sparse pieces of source material are placed far apart directly on the field: a compact paragraph, a small image fragment, and a single date. They are not inside cards or windows. A loose hand-drawn graphite loop groups the paragraph and image, demonstrating that spatial arrangement and marks steer a local language model. The model reacts only beside that group: a pale local halo, two light marginal notes, and one small proposed useful artifact. Everywhere else remains quiet. No permanent interface chrome, no navigation, no buttons, no chat box, no assistant character. Pencil on paper, precise editorial typography, subtle paper grain, graphite gray and black with one restrained cobalt accent, straight-on desktop landscape product visualization. Avoid dashboard, panels, floating card grid, toolbar, sidebar, glassmorphism, gradients, heavy shadows, neon, sci-fi HUD, device frame, excessive labels, illegible text.
