# Channing Court Concept Slides

## Slide 1: Site Signal

1601 Channing Ave is a real Palo Alto site with a 1951 single-story modern home, R1 zoning, and a 7,125 sf lot. The opportunity is not a teardown fantasy; it is a live preservation-and-capacity project that can test HII's QR-backed community funding layer.

Visual: listing/site photo, aerial, and one-line site facts.

## Slide 2: The Bet

Keep the Eichler street identity intact while making the property more useful: restored atrium, all-electric service spine, rear garden room, and a public project page where neighbors, investors, renters, and contractors can signal interest before capital is fully committed.

Visual: preserved front silhouette plus rear pavilion massing.

## Slide 3: The Plan

Three zones:

- Existing home: family/co-living plan and restored indoor/outdoor life.
- Service spine: electrification, storage, laundry, work wall, resilience systems.
- Garden room: office, guest suite, or ADU-ready leaseable studio.

Visual: site-plan.svg.

## Slide 4: Why Eichler Rules Matter

The concept respects single-story character, low rooflines, privacy, and tree sensitivity. The design avoids the main neighborhood conflict: bulky second-story additions that overlook glass-walled homes and break the tract rhythm.

Visual: "Do / Do Not" diagram.

## Slide 5: Funding Layer

The QR sign turns the site into an interactive project object:

- Vote on options.
- Pre-book workspace or ADU interest.
- Sponsor electrification or landscape packages.
- Invite contractor bids.
- Track budget movement and receipts.

Visual: QR sign at street edge connected to HII project dashboard.

## Slide 6: Option Ladder

Option A: Refresh only, fastest entitlement path.  
Option B: Refresh + service spine + landscape.  
Option C: Full rear garden-room/ADU-ready pavilion.

Visual: three massing thumbnails and budget ranges.
