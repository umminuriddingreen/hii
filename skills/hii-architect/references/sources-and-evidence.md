# Sources And Evidence

## Evidence Classes

Classify every material claim as `sourced`, `observed`, `calculated`, `assumed`, `inferred`, `illustrative`, or `unknown`.

## Authority Order

Prefer enacted/adopted law and official agencies; surveys and owner-authorized project records; official standards and manufacturer technical documents; primary project documentation; reputable secondary sources; then listings, blogs, social posts, and visual aggregators.

Record publisher, title, URL or local path, retrieval date, jurisdiction/version, access type, extracted fact, and affected claim IDs. Re-retrieve unstable sources before consequential use.

## Jurisdictional Rules

Resolve authority, adopted code editions, local amendments, zoning, overlays, parcel, and project scope at runtime. Translate each rule into source/section, applicability, required inputs, deterministic check, result, interpretation risk, and reviewer. Never mark the whole project compliant because a subset passed.

## Precedent Intelligence

Define the design question before searching. Mine context, program, organizing diagram, plan, section, circulation, structure, envelope, climate response, materials, construction, operations, dimensions, and transferable principle.

Separate fact from interpretation. Convert evidence into a design move plus the project constraint it addresses.

For Pinterest, Cosmos, or another logged-in library, require operator-authorized visible scope; inspect only approved pages or official APIs; never extract cookies, tokens, private messages, hidden profile data, or unrelated boards; and do not like, follow, save, comment, upload, purchase, or message without explicit authority.

## Confidence

- `high`: current authoritative source plus deterministic validated result; material assumptions satisfied.
- `medium`: authoritative source with partial validation or non-critical assumptions.
- `low`: stale/secondary source, inferred geometry, missing inputs, failed validation, or unresolved jurisdiction.

Confidence is claim-specific. A polished render does not raise confidence in geometry, code, budget, or constructability.
